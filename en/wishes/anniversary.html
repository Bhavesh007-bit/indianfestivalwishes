Writing an anniversary message

An anniversary celebrates a journey shared. Thank your parents for their years together, compliment your friends on their bond, and for your own partner, a shared memory always works best.

Wishes to send

For everyone

  • May your bond stay strong forever. Happy Anniversary!
  • May your journey of love and trust grow more beautiful each year.
  • Wishing you many more years of togetherness.

For a friend

  • You two make us believe in love. Happy Anniversary!
  • Keep laughing and bickering together. Congratulations!
  • Cheers to my favourite couple!

For family

  • Mom and Dad, your bond is our biggest inspiration. Happy Anniversary!
  • May your love and blessings always be with us.
  • May the home you built stay full of love.

For brother/sister

  • To my brother/sister and their better half, stay happy always!
  • May your love grow deeper every year.
  • Congratulations to our family's sweetest couple.

For husband/wife

  • Every year with you is better than the last. Happy Anniversary, my love!
  • I promise to hold your hand through every turn of life.
  • Meeting you was the best decision of my life.

For business/customers

  • Warm anniversary wishes to you and your spouse.
  • May your married life be full of joy and prosperity.
  • Congratulations from our entire team.

Devotional

  • May your bond be as unbreakable as Shiva and Parvati's.
  • May God always bless your union.
  • May love like Radha and Krishna's fill your life.
