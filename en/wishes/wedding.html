Congratulating the newlyweds

Wedding wishes bless a new beginning with love and companionship. If you can't attend, a personalised card is a lovely way to send your blessings.

Wishes to send

For everyone

  • Best wishes as you begin your new life together.
  • May your journey be filled with love, trust and joy.
  • May the seven vows make your life beautiful.

For a friend

  • You finally got married! Best wishes for the journey ahead.
  • Stay this happy forever, my friend.
  • I'll be the one dancing the most at your wedding. Congratulations!

For family

  • Welcome to our family! Happy married life.
  • May the blessings of elders always be with you.
  • May this bond between two families stay full of love.

For brother/sister

  • My dear brother/sister, congratulations on your new life!
  • Seeing you this happy fills my heart.
  • New home, new partner, but our love stays the same.

For business/customers

  • Warm wishes on your wedding from all of us.
  • May your married life be full of joy and success.
  • Congratulations to the newlyweds from our whole team.

Devotional

  • May Lord Ganesha remove every obstacle from your new life.
  • May your bond be as ideal as Sita and Ram's.
  • May God's blessings fill your home forever.
