How to send a birthday wish

Even a short message can make someone's day. Always use their name, and add something only the two of you share. Light humour works for friends, gratitude for parents, and warm, respectful words for customers.

Photo cards work well for status updates and stories; cards without a photo are great for group chats. Sending at midnight? Make the card in advance.

Wishes to send

For everyone

  • Wishing you a day as bright as your smile. Happy Birthday!
  • A new year of hope, joy and success awaits you.
  • Keep smiling, always. Many happy returns of the day!

For a friend

  • To my favourite partner in crime, may all your wishes come true!
  • Here's to our friendship and many more birthday parties together.
  • I'll be by your side at every milestone. Happy Birthday, buddy!

For family

  • You are the light of our home. Happy Birthday!
  • May God bless you with a long, healthy life.
  • May your love and blessings always stay with us.

For brother/sister

  • To my brother/sister and my biggest strength, Happy Birthday!
  • All our fights aside, home is incomplete without you.
  • May every day be as sweet as our childhood memories.

For husband/wife

  • Thank you for making my life beautiful. Happy Birthday, my love!
  • Every birthday, I love you more than the last.
  • Your smile is the best gift I've ever had.

For business/customers

  • Warm birthday wishes from all of us. Your trust means everything.
  • May the year ahead bring you success and prosperity.
  • Thank you for being part of our family. Happy Birthday!

Devotional

  • May God guide you at every step. Happy Birthday!
  • May the Lord bless you with happiness, peace and good health.
  • May God hear every prayer and grant every wish.
