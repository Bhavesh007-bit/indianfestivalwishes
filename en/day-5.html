Day 5

Maa Skandamata

The fifth day belongs to Maa Skandamata, the form that stands for a mother's love and care.

Date
Thursday, 15 October 2026
Colour of the day
Yellow
Bhog (offering)
Bananas

Make a greeting card with your name

Send on WhatsApp

The story of the goddess

Skanda is another name for Lord Kartikeya, and as his mother the goddess is called Skandamata. Kartikeya became commander of the gods' army and defeated the demon Tarakasura. The infant Skanda sits in her lap. She is seated on a lotus, which is why she is also called Padmasana Devi, and her vehicle is the lion. Worshipping her is believed to bless families with children, and the young Kartikeya is honoured along with her.

How to do the puja

Offer yellow flowers and bananas as bhog. Have the children of the family sit with you for the puja and seek blessings for them. Light a lamp, chant the mantra and perform the aarti.

Mantra

ॐ देवी स्कन्दमातायै नमः

Om Devi Skandamatayai Namah

Wishes to send

  • May Maa Skandamata protect your family from every trouble with her love. Wishes on the fifth day of Navratri!

  • As a mother holds her child close, may Maa Skandamata hold you close. Jai Mata Di!

  • May the children in your home always have the goddess's blessings. Happy Navratri!
