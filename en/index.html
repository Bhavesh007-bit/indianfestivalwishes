About this site

Indian Festival Wishes explains India's major festivals in simple language: why each one is celebrated, the story behind it, how the puja is done, and what people eat while fasting. On every festival and occasion page you can make a greeting card with your name and photo and send it to family and friends.

The whole site is available in Hindi, Gujarati and English. Switch languages from the top right. Your name is only used on your own phone or computer to draw the card; we don't store it anywhere.
