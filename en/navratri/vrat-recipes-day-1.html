Day 1: Maa Shailputri

Sabudana Khichdi, Vrat Jeera Aloo, Makhana Kheer

Day 1Day 2Day 3Day 4Day 5Day 6Day 7Day 8Day 9
Number of people

Quantities are typical home-cooking estimates. Adjust a little to suit your appetite and taste.

1. Sabudana Khichdi

Breakfast, 20 min

Ingredients

  • Sabudana (sago)70 g
  • Boiled potato80 g
  • Roasted peanuts, coarsely crushed25 g
  • Ghee1 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds½ tsp
  • Green chilli, finely chopped1 pc
  • Rock salt (sendha namak)to taste
  • Lemon juice1 tsp
  • Coriander leaves, chopped1 tbsp

Method

  1. Rinse the sabudana 2-3 times and soak it for 5-6 hours in just enough water to cover it. It is ready when a pearl crushes easily between your fingers.
  2. Peel the potato and cut it into small cubes.
  3. Heat ghee in a pan, let the cumin splutter, then add the green chilli and potato and sauté for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the sabudana, peanuts and rock salt. Cook on low heat for 5-7 minutes, stirring, until the pearls turn translucent. Overcooking makes it sticky.
  5. Turn off the heat, mix in lemon juice and coriander, and serve hot.

2. Vrat Jeera Aloo

Main meal, 15 min

Ingredients

  • Boiled potato150 g
  • Ghee1 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds1 tsp
  • Green chilli, finely chopped1 pc
  • Ginger, grated5 g
  • Rock salt (sendha namak)to taste
  • Black pepper powder¼ tsp
  • Lemon juice1 tsp
  • Coriander leaves, chopped1 tbsp

Method

  1. Peel the boiled potatoes and cut them into medium cubes.
  2. Heat ghee in a pan, let the cumin turn golden, then sauté the green chilli and ginger for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the potatoes, rock salt and pepper. Toss on medium-high heat for 4-5 minutes until the edges turn lightly golden.
  4. Finish with lemon juice and coriander. Serve with kuttu puri or curd.

3. Makhana Kheer

Sweet, 25 min

Ingredients

  • Makhana (fox nuts)20 g
  • Full-fat milk250 ml
  • Sugar20 g
  • Ghee1 tsp
  • Cardamom powder1 pinch
  • Chopped cashews, almonds, pistachios10 g

Method

  1. Heat ghee in a pan and roast the makhana on low heat for 3-4 minutes until crisp. Lightly crush half of them.
  2. Boil the milk in a heavy-bottomed pan and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the makhana and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and the kheer thickens.
  4. Add sugar and cardamom and cook 2 more minutes. Top with dry fruits and serve warm or chilled.
