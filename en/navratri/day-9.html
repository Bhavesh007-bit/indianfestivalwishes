Day 9

Maa Siddhidatri

The ninth day is Maha Navami. With the worship of Maa Siddhidatri, the nine days of Navratri come to completion.

Date
Monday, 19 October 2026
Colour of the day
Peacock green
Bhog (offering)
Sesame, and halwa-puri-chana

The story of the goddess

Siddhidatri means the one who grants siddhis, or spiritual powers. It is believed that even Lord Shiva gained his powers through her grace, and that half of his body became hers, giving him the form of Ardhanarishvara. She sits on a lotus and rides a lion, holding a discus, conch, mace and lotus in her four hands. Gods, sages and ordinary devotees all worship her. The fruit of all nine days of worship is considered complete on this day.

How to do the puja

Offer sesame seeds as bhog. Many families prepare halwa, puri and chana as prasad today and perform Kanya Pujan. Close Navratri with a havan, and break the fast the following day.

Mantra

ॐ देवी सिद्धिदात्र्यै नमः

Om Devi Siddhidatryai Namah
