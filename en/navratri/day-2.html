Day 2

Maa Brahmacharini

The second day is for Maa Brahmacharini, the goddess of penance and self-discipline. The day carries a message of hard work and patience.

Date
Monday, 12 October 2026
Colour of the day
White
Bhog (offering)
Sugar, mishri and fruit

The story of the goddess

To win Lord Shiva as her husband, Parvati performed intense penance for thousands of years. At first she lived on fruit and flowers, then on dry bel leaves, and finally gave up even leaves, which earned her the name Aparna. Her devotion moved even the gods. Maa Brahmacharini wears white, walks barefoot, and holds a prayer rosary in one hand and a water pot in the other. Worshipping her is believed to build self-control and dedication to one's goals.

How to do the puja

Bathe in the morning and wear white or light colours. Offer white flowers, rice grains and sandalwood. Offer sugar or mishri as bhog and share it with the family afterwards. Chant the mantra with a calm mind.

Mantra

ॐ देवी ब्रह्मचारिण्यै नमः

Om Devi Brahmacharinyai Namah
