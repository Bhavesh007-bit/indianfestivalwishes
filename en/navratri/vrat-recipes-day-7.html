Day 7: Maa Kalaratri

Shakarkand Chaat, Kuttu Cheela, Rajgira Jaggery Ladoo

Day 1Day 2Day 3Day 4Day 5Day 6Day 7Day 8Day 9
Number of people

Quantities are typical home-cooking estimates. Adjust a little to suit your appetite and taste.

1. Shakarkand Chaat

Evening snack, 20 min

Ingredients

  • Sweet potato150 g
  • Ghee1 tsp
  • Lemon juice2 tsp
  • Roasted cumin powder½ tsp
  • Rock salt (sendha namak)to taste
  • Black pepper powder1 pinch
  • Green chilli, finely chopped½ pc
  • Pomegranate seeds20 g
  • Coriander leaves, chopped1 tbsp

Method

  1. Wash the sweet potato and cook it in a pressure cooker for 2 whistles, or boil for about 15 minutes until soft.
  2. Once cool, peel and cut into cubes.
  3. Heat ghee and toast the cubes for 3 minutes until lightly golden (optional).
  4. Toss with lemon, roasted cumin, rock salt, pepper and green chilli. Top with pomegranate and coriander.

2. Kuttu Cheela

Breakfast, 20 min

Ingredients

  • Buckwheat (kuttu) flour50 g
  • Thick curd2 tbsp
  • Water60 ml
  • Boiled potato40 g
  • Green chilli, finely chopped1 pc
  • Coriander leaves, chopped1 tbsp
  • Rock salt (sendha namak)to taste
  • Ghee2 tsp

Method

  1. Whisk the kuttu flour with the curd and water into a smooth, thin, pourable batter.
  2. Stir in the grated boiled potato, green chilli, coriander and rock salt. Rest for 5 minutes.
  3. Heat a non-stick tawa and grease it lightly. Pour a ladle of batter and spread it thin in circles.
  4. Drizzle ghee around the edges and cook for about 2 minutes per side until golden. Serve with curd or coconut chutney.

3. Rajgira Jaggery Ladoo

Sweet, 20 min

Ingredients

  • Puffed amaranth30 g
  • Jaggery, grated25 g
  • Roasted peanuts, coarsely crushed10 g
  • Ghee1 tsp
  • Water1 tsp
  • Cardamom powder1 pinch

Method

  1. Grease your palms with ghee in advance, since the ladoos must be shaped while warm.
  2. Melt the ghee, jaggery and water in a pan on low heat. Cook for 3-4 minutes until frothy. Drop a little into cold water; if it hardens, the syrup is ready.
  3. Turn off the heat and quickly mix in the puffed rajgira, peanuts and cardamom.
  4. Shape 3-4 ladoos while still warm. Store once cool. Jaggery is Maa Kalaratri's bhog.
