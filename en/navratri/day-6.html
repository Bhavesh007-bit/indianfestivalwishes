Day 6

Maa Katyayani

The sixth day honours Maa Katyayani, who is also worshipped as Mahishasura Mardini, the slayer of Mahishasura.

Date
Friday, 16 October 2026
Colour of the day
Green
Bhog (offering)
Honey

The story of the goddess

The sage Katyayana performed penance hoping the goddess would be born as his daughter. Because she appeared in his ashram, she came to be called Katyayani. When the demon Mahishasura was tormenting the gods, Maa Katyayani fought him and killed him. It is believed that the gopis of Vrindavan worshipped her on the banks of the Yamuna, praying for Krishna as their husband. For this reason, she is also worshipped by those hoping for a good life partner. She rides a lion.

How to do the puja

Offer red or yellow flowers and honey as bhog. After chanting the mantra, place your wish before the goddess. Share the prasad after the evening aarti.

Mantra

ॐ देवी कात्यायन्यै नमः

Om Devi Katyayanyai Namah
